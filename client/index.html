FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How do I share files? +

Select or drag your files into the upload area and click Upload & Share. You will receive a share code and link.

Can I send multiple files? +

Yes. DropBox Local supports selecting multiple files in one transfer.

How does the receiver get my files? +

Send them your share code or link. They can enter the code in the Receive Files section.

Do transfers expire? +

Yes. Transfers are temporary and are automatically removed after their expiry period.
